TEKE FAITH JANE — UI/UX PORTFOLIO
================================

RUN THE PORTFOLIO
1. Open this folder in VS Code.
2. If needed, install the Live Server extension.
3. Right-click index.html and choose “Open with Live Server”.
   The portfolio opens in your browser. You can also open index.html directly.

EDIT YOUR CONTENT
- Edit the Home preview and introduction in index.html.
- Edit the full biography in about.html, skills/services and pricing in skills.html, process in approach.html, and contact details in contact.html.
- Social profile icons are ready in the footer; add your profile URLs when available.
- Edit project summaries, user flows and screen descriptions in script.js.
- Edit colors, layout and responsive styles in style.css.

YOUR PHOTO AND CV
Replace these files with your own, keeping the filenames:
assets/faith-jane-portrait.jpg
assets/faith-jane-cv.pdf
The CV button downloads the PDF. The portrait is cropped to its portrait frame without stretching.

The portfolio uses a project’s first screen as its card image, then falls back to that project’s cover. When an image is missing, the page shows its upload path. Images stay in their original proportions: portrait screens are scaled to fit the frame, while wide images use the available width. They are not stretched or cropped in project galleries.

CONTACT
The contact form checks the name, email and message, then opens a Gmail draft addressed to faithenjie4@gmail.com. The visitor reviews and sends the draft in Gmail. The email link and phone number also open their relevant apps. Automatic background email delivery needs a configured email service or server.
