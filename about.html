DESIGNING

I am a full-stack software engineer, UI/UX and product designer, print-on-demand designer, writer, and HR and administrative specialist based in Yaoundé, Cameroon. With a BSc in Software Engineering, I bring together thoughtful design, practical software skills, and experience supporting people and operations.

I care about the moment a confusing process becomes clear. QueueLess started with a simple observation: people lose hours waiting without knowing when they’ll be served. I explored how virtual queues, live position updates and appointment booking could give people more control over that time.

That same curiosity guides projects across healthcare, agriculture, food, housing, personal styling and grocery shopping. I always start by understanding people and the problem, then shape a clear, useful experience with care for all details.

My work spans digital infrastructure, intern operations, executive support, and web and mobile application development. I enjoy connecting business needs with dependable software and clear communication. I bring organization, discretion, adaptability, careful problem solving, and strong written and verbal communication. I speak English fluently and French at a professional working level. My final-year AgriTracker project combines Python crop diagnostics with Gemini and OpenAI APIs and projects a 25% reduction in post-harvest losses. Explore AgriTracker.
